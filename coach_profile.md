# Coach Profile & Training Philosophy

**Athlete Context:** 147–153 lbs (66.7–69.4 kg) | **FTP:** 365 W (~5.26–5.47 W/kg)  
**Data Analyzed:** 2025 Season (Mar 1 – Sep 30, 2025) & 2026 Season (Feb 20 – Jul 23, 2026)

---

## 1. Core Coaching Philosophy
This coach uses a **high-volume, durability-first European road-racing model** built around a pyramidal-to-polarized intensity profile. Intervals are almost never isolated into short 60-minute trainer sessions; instead, quality work is consistently embedded inside **3h–5h rides** to train fatigue resistance, late-race repeatability, and kermis/stage-race surges.

---

## 2. Periodization
The coach follows a **traditional linear progression into race-specific specialization**, punctuated by **3:1 loading/deloading rhythms** and periodic max-effort field tests (`1x8 min` / `1x5 min` uphill max, or a `35 min` progressive climb ending in `10 min` full gas).

* **Late Base / Strength-Endurance (Feb – Early Apr):**
  * **2025:** Heavy focus on low-cadence uphill torque (`320W @ 50 rpm`) and 5h endurance rides.
  * **2026:** Shifted toward high-volume aerobic tempo (`2x20m` → `4x20m` → `5x20m @ 300W`, ~82% FTP) paired with uphill VO2 surges (`2m @ 440W / 1m @ 250W`).
* **Build Phase (Mid-Apr – Early Jun):**
  * Introduces **over-unders**, **micro-intervals** (`30/15s`, `40/20s`), **split-set VO2/sprints**, and **front-loaded durability rides** (intervals in Hour 1, followed by 3–4h of steady Z2).
* **Peak / Race-Specific Specialization (Mid-Jun – Early Aug 2025):**
  * Sharpens both ends of the spectrum simultaneously: massive **Sweet Spot durability** (`3x20m` → `4x30m` → `2x40m` → `2x60m @ 320–340W`) alongside **supramaximal anaerobic/VO2 blocks** (`440–520W` for 2–4 min, `600–800W` for 30–60 sec).
* **In-Season / Racing & Maintenance (Aug–Sep 2025 & Jun–Jul 2026):**
  * Volume drops sharply around heavy racing (e.g., Belgium kermis block). Training strips down to `1.5h coffee/recovery rides`, `4h easy endurance`, and light `2h` maintenance/opener sessions (`3x4–5m @ 330W` or `5m @ 370–410W + 2x1m @ 450–500W + 3x8s sprints`).

---

## 3. Weekly Volume
* **Standard Build / Peak Weeks:** **18–23.5 planned hours/week** across 5–7 days (logged completion typically 14–19h).
* **Deload / Testing Weeks (every ~3–4 weeks):** **11–14 planned hours/week** (~30–40% volume reduction, capped at 2h–2h30 rides).
* **In-Season / Heavy Racing Weeks:** **8–13 planned hours/week** of non-race riding (plus races).
* **Ride Duration Anchors:** Almost all structured sessions are **3h–4h**; pure endurance days are **4h–5h**; easy/recovery days are strictly **1h–1h30**.

---

## 4. Intensity Distribution
**Model:** **Pyramidal with Polarized/Durability accents** (~75–80% Z1–Z2, ~12–15% Z3/Sweet Spot, ~8–12% Z4–Z6).

* **Strict Low-Intensity Discipline:** Pure endurance and recovery rides are genuinely easy (`0.40–0.56 IF` on recovery/coffee rides; `0.50–0.65 IF` on 4h–5h Z2 rides). The coach explicitly cues *"just easy, don't force it, we need some low intensity work."*
* **Massive Tempo / Sweet Spot Engine:** Rather than classic 2x20 FTP blocks, the coach builds threshold from below using huge volumes of **Tempo (`290–300W`, 79–82% FTP)** and **Sweet Spot (`320–340W`, 88–93% FTP)**—progressing all the way to `100–120 min` of total work in a single ride.
* **Stochastic High Intensity:** Above threshold, steady-state intervals are rare. Almost all Z4–Z6 work uses **surges, ramps, over-unders, or micro-intervals** (`370–550W`, 101–150% FTP) to mirror road-race dynamics.

## 6. Hard / Easy Day Sequencing
The coach avoids strict "hard/easy/hard/easy" alternation during build blocks, preferring **clustered overload** followed by deliberate recovery:

1. **Base Pattern (5 days/week — Tue–Thu & Sat–Sun):**
   * **Mon:** Full Rest
   * **Tue:** Hard #1 (Torque or VO2/Over-Unders, 3–3.5h)
   * **Wed:** Hard #2 (Back-to-back quality: Surge SST, Progressive 7x8m, or Sprints, 3–4h)
   * **Thu:** Long Endurance (`5h` Z2 on tired legs)
   * **Fri:** Full Rest
   * **Sat:** Hard #3 (Torque or Intervals, 3–3.5h)
   * **Sun:** Long Endurance (`4.5–5h` Z2)
2. **Build / Peak Pattern (6–7 days/week — 2-to-3 Day Load Clusters):**
   * Replaces full rest days with **`1h–1h30` active recovery / coffee rides** (or fun MTB/gravel).
   * Frequently stacks **2–3 consecutive hard days** (e.g., *Anaerobic 2-min efforts → Sweet Spot 3x20m → VO2 4x3m*, OR *VO2/Over-Unders → 4h w/ 5x20m @ 300W → 5h Endurance*), then inserts a `1h–1h30` easy day before repeating.
   * **Hard → Long Rule:** Almost every major intensity cluster is capped with a **4h–5h endurance ride** immediately after (rather than before) to maximize aerobic adaptation under glycogen depletion.
3. **In-Season / Racing Pattern:**
   * **Post-Race:** `1h–1h30` recovery/coffee ride (often 2 easy days after hard race weekends).
   * **Mid-Week:** One `4h easy endurance` ride + one crisp maintenance session (`2h w/ 3x4m @ 330W`).
   * **Pre-Race (Day -1 or -2):** `1h30` openers or `1h` easy + short sprints.
